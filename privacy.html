Penwise

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 23, 2026

In short: Penwise edits your PDFs entirely inside your browser. Your documents, signatures and form entries never leave your computer. We don't use analytics or ads, and we don't sell data. Penwise has no accounts and no payments, and it doesn't contact any server of ours.

Your documents

When you open a PDF in Penwise, it is read, displayed, edited and saved on your own device using code that ships with the extension. Its contents, including anything you type, draw or sign, are never sent to us or to anyone else.

PDFs opened from websites

If you ask Penwise to open a PDF that's on a website, Chrome asks your permission for that site. Penwise then downloads that one file directly from the site into the editor, just as your browser would. Nothing is routed through our servers.

Data stored on your device

  • Saved signatures: if you leave "Remember on this device" ticked, your signature image is stored in Chrome's local extension storage so you can reuse it. You can delete saved signatures in the signature window at any time, and uninstalling Penwise removes them.
  • My details: if you save your name, address, email or phone so you can drop them onto forms, they are stored in Chrome's local extension storage on your device. You can edit or delete them at any time.
  • Autosave: while you work, Penwise keeps a copy of your unsaved changes on your device so it can offer to restore them if the tab closes by accident. This copy never leaves your computer. You can discard it when Penwise offers to restore it, and uninstalling Penwise removes it.

What we don't do

  • We don't collect analytics, usage tracking or browsing history.
  • We don't show ads.
  • We don't sell, rent or share your data.
  • We don't use data for anything other than making Penwise work.

Permissions Penwise uses

  • Context menus: adds "Fill & sign this PDF with Penwise" to the right-click menu.
  • Storage: keeps saved signatures, your saved details and autosaved work on your device.
  • Website access (optional, per site): only requested when you open a PDF from that site, to download it.

Children

Penwise is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date above. Significant changes will also be noted in the extension's release notes.
