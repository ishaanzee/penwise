How to fill out a PDF form for free, without a surprise paywall

A common trap: you spend twenty minutes typing into an online PDF editor, click Download, and only then get asked for a subscription. Here's how to avoid it, and which free tools actually let you save your work.

Updated October 2026 · 4 min read

First: is your PDF "fillable"?

Open the PDF and click on a blank line. If you can type straight into a box, it's a fillable form and almost any PDF viewer can fill it. If clicking does nothing, it's a flat PDF: a picture of a form. You'll need a tool that lets you place text boxes anywhere on the page.

How to avoid the "pay to download" trap

  • Check the download terms before you start. Look for a pricing page or "free" plan limits (number of documents, watermarks, file size).
  • Try saving after typing one field. If downloading is blocked, you've lost one minute, not twenty.
  • Prefer tools that work offline or in your browser. They usually can't hold your file hostage, because they never have it.
  • Read recent reviews in the app or extension store. Complaints like "made me pay after filling" are common and easy to spot.
